How Radia Works

1. Live World Map & Adaptive Sprawl Radius Switch between 5 free global map repositories (`Street & Park`, `OSM Detail`, `Minimal Light`, `Topographic`, `Evening Dark`) or jump between cities (`NYC`, `SF`, `London`, `Austin`, `Tokyo`, or `Live GPS`). Your proximity bubble automatically scales from `18ft` in dense urban cores to `65ft` on open trails.
2. Walking Telemetry & Community Hubs Tap anywhere on the world map to walk there, or use Auto-Walk / Live GPS. Walking earns Field Credits and unlocks distance benchmarks (`0.25km` to `1.0 Mile`).
3. Mutual Insights & AI Synthesis Graph After each conversation, both participants share a thoughtful takeaway. Your insights connect in the **Insight Graph**, tracking spatial clusters (including *10 Insights in the Same Mile*) and synthesizing how you've changed, what you've learned, and your effect on people around you.
