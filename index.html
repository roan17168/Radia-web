Product Guide

How Radia Works

1. You are the centre of the map Radia anchors on your real GPS position. Your conversation bubble is calibrated to the place you are standing — tight in a dense street, wider on an open trail — and it grows only when real people join you. Map styles are free and need no API key.
2. Walking earns credit Start a walk and Radia records the distance you actually travel. Every hundred metres earns Field Credits and moves your distance goals along. Nothing is simulated, and tapping the map never mints credit.
3. Every conversation leaves an insight When a conversation ends, both people offer one thoughtful thing they took from it. Those insights become your insight graph — linked to the prompt and the place they happened — so you can see how you've changed, what you've learned, and your effect on the people around you.
